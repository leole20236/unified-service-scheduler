(~25s) Here's how a customer books. First, they see free times from Redis. Then they pick one, and we hold it for ten minutes, like a seat on a plane. If someone else already has it, they get other times. Then they confirm. The database saves everything in one step, and it refuses any overlap. Retries are safe, so nobody books twice.
